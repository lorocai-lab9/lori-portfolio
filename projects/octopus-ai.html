Problem Statement

Strong differentiators. Classic adoption barriers.

The model had real edges — speed, privacy, on-device processing — but suffered the standard AI cold-start problem: users couldn't internalize the value fast enough. Drop-off happened before activation.

Our Solution

A focused Playground that lets the model's speed, privacy, and locality speak through interaction — not slides.

A web experience designed to turn first-look curiosity into real adoption — for both business leaders and hands-on developers.

MVP Flow

The simplest experience that reveals the model's edge.

Pick an industry → browse the playground → copy a preset prompt → see the model advantages. Four steps, deliberately undone — every layer of polish would have eaten the deadline.

SCHOOL Tech-savvy Newcomer Designer PM Researcher Founder Engineer
Personas

Two audiences, two on-ramps.

Tech-savvy professionals wanted code-level access and deep documentation. Practical newcomers from business and design needed a no-friction first impression. The same product had to land twice.

Iteration

Different audiences, different on-ramps.

For non-technical users: redesigned IA, optimized visualization, refined copy. Researched four popular industries and prototyped video scenarios showing edge-AI experiences in context.

For technical users: comprehensive documentation matching developer expectations, plus revised playground interaction logic for model selection and execution.

Reflection

When a technology is new, clarity matters more than completeness.

By prioritizing how users naturally evaluate models — through direct interaction — we reduced friction, accelerated team alignment, and shipped meaningful results under extreme constraints.
